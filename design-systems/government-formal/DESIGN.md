# Government Formal

> The aesthetic of a competent civil service: GOV.UK / USDS / 政务 portal.
> Formal but not pompous, accessible by mandate, trustworthy by virtue of
> restraint. Use for municipal services, regulatory portals, NGO reports,
> civic information.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Sober, civic, factual. Type does the work. There is no marketing copy —
every page is informational. Imagery is documentary photography or simple
flat illustration in two colours.

## Color Palette & Roles
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface 2 (banner):** `#0B2E5C` (formal navy)
- **Foreground:** `#0E0E0E`
- **Muted:** `#5A5A5A`
- **Accent (state):** `#A12B2B` (state red — for the masthead emblem and one rule per page)
- **Border:** `#D8D8D8`
- **Link:** `#0B2E5C` underlined

## Typography
- **Display (masthead):** "Source Serif", "Tinos", "Cambria", "Cormorant" — readable serif at 700
- **Body:** "Source Sans 3", "Inter", "Helvetica" at 400, 17px
- Headings are sentence case, not Title Case (mirrors GOV.UK).
- Body NEVER uses italic for body content; italic is reserved for citations.

## Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** solid green (`#1F8E5C` — gov-green) for primary actions ("Continue", "Submit"), outlined navy for secondary. 4px radius (just enough to not feel raw).
- **Cards:** white background with 1px hairline border, 0–8px radius.
- **Banners:** full-bleed navy bar at the top of the page with the state emblem.
- **Links:** always underlined, both in body and in nav. Visited links shift colour subtly.
- **Tables:** zebra-striped permitted; rules-only also acceptable.

## Layout Principles
- Strict 12-column grid; content respects the grid without exception.
- Single-column body content (max 64ch) for readability.
- Generous whitespace between sections (96px+).
- Top of page: navy banner with state emblem; below it, formal serif H1.

## Depth & Elevation
None. Civic = flat. No shadows, no playful gradients.

## Accessibility (non-negotiable)
- WCAG AA minimum, AAA preferred for text.
- Skip-to-content link at top of page.
- Form labels visible at all times.
- All interactive elements have visible focus rings.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** use sentence case for headings.
- **Do** include a navy top banner with the state emblem.
- **Do** underline every link.
- **Don't** use marketing language. Plain, factual writing only.
- **Don't** use rounded corners larger than 8px.
- **Don't** use gradients or glass effects.

## Responsive Behavior
Single-column body throughout. Top banner shrinks but persists.

## Agent Prompt Guide
1. Add a full-bleed navy banner at the top with the state emblem.
2. Use a serif masthead and sans-serif body, both readable.
3. Sentence case for all headings, never Title Case.
4. Use solid green for primary action buttons.
5. All links underlined, both in body and navigation.
