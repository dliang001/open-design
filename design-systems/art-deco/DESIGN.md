# Art Deco

> The Chrysler Building, Erté posters, Gatsby's chandelier. Strict
> symmetry, geometric ornament, condensed display serifs over cream or
> black. Use for theatre programmes, hospitality flagships, jewellery,
> perfume, premium event invitations.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Formal, ornate, restrained-luxurious. Compositions are mirror-symmetric.
The page reads like an etched announcement card.

## Color Palette & Roles
- **Background:** `#F2EAD3` (warm cream) — primary
- **Background dark variant:** `#0E0E0E` (true black) — for night-mode / hero panels
- **Foreground:** `#0E0E0E` on cream / `#F2EAD3` on black
- **Gold (primary accent):** `#C9A961` — ornaments, rules, dividers
- **Burgundy (rare secondary):** `#5C1F2A` — small editorial accents only
- **Bronze (foliage):** `#7A5C3F` — supporting tone

## Typography
- **Display:** "Playfair Display Black", "Bodoni Bold", "Didot Bold" — high-contrast serif at heavy weight, condensed if available
- **Sub-display:** condensed sans like "Bebas Neue" for kicker labels
- **Body:** "Cormorant Garamond" or "EB Garamond" at 17–19px, 1.6 line-height
- Tracking is wide on ALL CAPS labels (+8 to +12%)
- Centre-alignment is permitted (and expected) for invitation-style headers

## Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** centred all-caps text inside a thin gold rule rectangle, no radius. Hover: fill with gold, invert text.
- **Cards:** symmetric layout, gold hairline frame with corner ornaments (small SVG sunburst / fan).
- **Section dividers:** centred geometric ornament (sunburst, chevron stack, lozenge) flanked by gold rules.
- **Imagery:** monochrome (sepia / black-and-white), oval or rectangular gold-rule frames.

## Layout Principles
- Mirror symmetry across the vertical axis. Centre is sacred.
- Hierarchy by ornamental weight (a gold rule under a heading carries weight, not boldness).
- Section spacing is theatrical — 160–240px on desktop.

## Depth & Elevation
None. Depth is implied by metallic gold against flat cream / black.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** centre headings.
- **Do** use a gold rule and a small ornament under section headers.
- **Do** keep imagery monochrome.
- **Don't** introduce a third colour beyond gold and burgundy.
- **Don't** use modern shadows or rounded corners.
- **Don't** left-align hero content.

## Responsive Behavior
Symmetry preserved on mobile; ornaments scale down but never disappear.

## Agent Prompt Guide
1. Centre-align all hero and section headers.
2. Use gold (`#C9A961`) for rules, frames, and ornaments.
3. Add a small geometric ornament (sunburst / chevron stack) below each section title.
4. Imagery is sepia / black-and-white only.
5. Display type is high-contrast serif at heavy weight; condensed if available.
