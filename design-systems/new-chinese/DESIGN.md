# New Chinese (新中式)

> Contemporary translation of traditional Chinese aesthetics: 留白 (negative
> space), ink-wash gradients, the vermillion seal as the only colour
> accent, and song / sans pairings. For tea brands, hospitality, museums,
> high-end real estate, and culture-led marketing in CN markets.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Restrained, contemplative, breathing. Composition borrows from scroll
paintings and shanshui — large empty zones with elements anchored low or
to one corner.

## Color Palette & Roles
- **Background:** `#F5F0E8` (rice paper / 宣纸)
- **Foreground:** `#1F1B14` (ink black) — body and headings
- **Muted:** `#807060` (aged ink)
- **Vermillion (seal):** `#A52A2A` (印泥红) — used ONCE per page like a seal-stamp on a scroll
- **Jade green (rare):** `#3A6B58` — for one editorial element when an alternative to vermillion is needed

Vermillion is treated as a stamp, not a CTA colour: small, deliberate, end-of-section.

## Typography
- **Display (CJK):** "Source Han Serif" (思源宋体) / "Noto Serif CJK SC" at heavy weight
- **Display (Latin):** "Noto Serif", "Source Serif" — Latin should mirror the CJK style
- **Body:** "Source Han Sans" / "Noto Sans CJK SC" at regular weight, 16–17px
- Mix display serif with body sans, never the reverse
- Vertical writing for one decorative element (`writing-mode: vertical-rl`) is encouraged

## Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** outlined rectangle with 1px ink stroke, no fill, no radius. Hover fills with ink (foreground colour) and inverts text.
- **Cards:** rice-paper background with 1px aged-ink border, generous padding (40px+).
- **Section dividers:** ink-brush horizontal stroke (SVG with rough edges).
- **Imagery:** 4:3 or 4:5 ratio, slight desaturation, occasional ink-wash overlay.

## Layout Principles
- Composition leaves a "diagonal void" — large negative space sloping from one corner.
- Type is clustered tightly in one corner; rest of the page breathes.
- Strict vertical alignment baseline: every block aligns to a single ruled line on either left or right margin.

## Depth & Elevation
Flat. Depth implied by overlapping ink-wash gradients (light grey → cream).

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** leave 40%+ of the screen as negative space.
- **Do** anchor copy to one corner and let the rest breathe.
- **Do** use vermillion as a "seal" only at section ends.
- **Don't** centre everything — composition is asymmetric.
- **Don't** use saturated brand colours; ink and seal-red only.
- **Don't** add rounded corners or modern shadows.

## Responsive Behavior
Negative space scales proportionally. The diagonal void principle is preserved on mobile.

## Agent Prompt Guide
1. Use rice-paper background `#F5F0E8`, never white.
2. Pair Source Han Serif (CJK) with Source Serif (Latin) for headings.
3. Anchor every section's content to one corner, leaving the opposite half empty.
4. Use vermillion `#A52A2A` exactly once per section as a seal-stamp.
5. Section spacing 128–160px.
