# Nordic Hygge

> The Aesop / Frama / Norm Architects feeling. Warmth without colour,
> calm without sterility. Use for wellness brands, indie hospitality,
> sustainable consumer goods, slow-fashion, indie cafes.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Quiet but lived-in. Warmer than Muji, less monastic. Photography is warm
natural light, slightly desaturated, with single-subject framing.

## Color Palette & Roles
- **Background:** `#EFE8DC` (warm oat paper)
- **Surface 2:** `#FAF6EE` (lighter card)
- **Foreground:** `#23201B` (charcoal-warm)
- **Muted:** `#6F6657` (warm grey)
- **Border:** `#D3CABB`
- **Accent (sage):** `#7B8762` — primary accent, used for callouts and one CTA per section
- **Accent (rust):** `#9E5B3D` — used as a second accent for one element (badge, kicker)

## Typography
- **Display:** "Söhne", "Inter Tight", "GT America" — a friendly geometric sans at 500–600 weight, NOT extralight or extrabold
- **Body:** same family at 400, 17px, 1.65 line-height
- **Optional editorial serif:** "Lora" or "Source Serif" used sparingly for pull quotes
- Tracking: normal. Sentence case for headings.

## Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** rounded pill (24px radius), soft sage fill for primary; outlined sage for secondary.
- **Cards:** soft 16px radius, 1px hairline warm border, 32px internal padding.
- **Imagery:** 4:5 ratio, warm-toned, soft 8px radius corners (rare exception to most-paper systems).
- **Section dividers:** thin warm-grey rule.

## Layout Principles
- Single-column hero with image right, text left (or vice versa).
- Generous outer margins (96–128px on desktop).
- Section spacing 96–144px.
- One image per section is the rule.

## Depth & Elevation
- Very soft shadows, warm-tinted: `0 2px 8px rgba(35,32,27,0.06)`.
- Cards "rest on paper" — no glass, no glow.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** use the warm oat background; never pure white.
- **Do** keep one image per section.
- **Do** use sage as the primary accent; rust used very rarely.
- **Don't** use saturated colours.
- **Don't** over-tighten margins; the breath is the brand.
- **Don't** use heavy or extralight type weights — friendly middle weights only.

## Responsive Behavior
Single column on mobile. Image-text pairs stack with image first. Margins compress to 24px.

## Agent Prompt Guide
1. Use warm oat paper background `#EFE8DC`.
2. Body type is friendly geometric sans at 17px.
3. Primary CTA is sage-filled pill with 24px radius.
4. Use rust accent on at most ONE element per page (badge or kicker).
5. Pair imagery 4:5 with 8px radius and warm tones.
