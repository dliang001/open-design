# Y2K Digital

> The aesthetic of WinAmp skins, Lisa Frank planners, and the early iMac.
> Use for nostalgia drops, Gen-Z marketing, fashion pop-ups, music brand
> moments. Wears thin if used for serious B2B.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Saturated pastels meet metallic chrome. Bubbly type, slightly tilted layouts,
glossy surfaces. Joyful, optimistic, unmistakably "online".

## Color Palette & Roles
- **Background:** `#F0F4FF` (icy blue-white)
- **Foreground:** `#1A0A4D` (deep indigo) — body text
- **Accent (primary):** `#7B5CFA` (lavender) — CTAs, links
- **Accent (secondary):** `#FF7AB6` (bubblegum) — highlights, active states
- **Accent (tertiary):** `#75F0C2` (mint) — success, badges
- **Chrome gradient:** linear-gradient(180deg, `#E8ECFF`, `#B8C4FF`) — used on cards, pills

## Typography
- **Display:** "Bubble", "VAG Rounded", "Avenir Black" — fall back to a heavy rounded sans
- **Body:** "Inter", "Helvetica Neue", system sans at 16px
- Italics are in. Tracking is wide on display headlines (+2 to +4%).

## Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** rounded-full pill, chrome gradient fill, 1px white inner border, soft drop-shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(123,92,250,0.4)`. Hover: scale 1.04, brighter gradient.
- **Cards:** 16px border radius, white fill, 2px lavender border, soft glossy highlight at top edge.
- **Inputs:** 12px radius, white fill, lavender 2px border on focus.
- **Stickers:** rotated 4–8°, drop-shadow, used as decorative accents on hero sections.

## Layout Principles
- Hero with off-axis stickers / shapes — composition feels collaged, not gridded.
- Body sections settle into a calmer 12-column grid.
- Generous border-radius everywhere (12–24px).

## Depth & Elevation
- Soft drop shadows tinted with the lavender / pink palette.
- Inner highlights ("glossy strip" at the top edge of cards).
- One accent element may have a subtle pulse animation (1.5s ease-in-out).

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** mix two pastels in the same hero section.
- **Do** use a single rotated sticker / badge per page for personality.
- **Don't** use more than three accents on the same screen.
- **Don't** use sharp corners. 12px radius minimum.
- **Don't** use neutral grey — the palette is fully saturated.

## Responsive Behavior
Stickers shrink and re-anchor to corners on mobile. Two-column layouts collapse to single column.

## Agent Prompt Guide
1. Use rounded-full pill buttons with chrome gradients.
2. Apply minimum 12px border-radius to every container.
3. Add ONE rotated decorative sticker / badge per hero.
4. Use lavender for primary CTAs, bubblegum for secondary highlights.
5. Drop-shadows are tinted (purple/pink), never neutral grey.
